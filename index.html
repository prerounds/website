Inside a study room

Cameras on, heads down, a timer everyone shares. It feels like the library table where you actually got work done.

How a session works

  1. Choose where to sit

    Drop in at an open table on any floor, or book a room ahead of time.

  2. Get matched

    Partners are paired by time, format, social level, and stage of training.

  3. Check in

    State your goal in fifteen seconds when you sit down, then mark it done when you leave.

  4. Keep the record

    Hours and goals are logged for you, so progress over weeks is easy to see.

Free. No card. No catch.

Nothing to pay

Study rooms, matching, check-ins, and tracking are free for students. There is no paid tier to unlock.

No card to join

Signing up takes your email and nothing else. We never ask for a credit card or payment details.

No cancellation fees

Miss a session and nothing is charged. A reliability score for showing up keeps everyone accountable instead.

Safe by design

Members only

Every member verifies their email before joining a room. No anonymous drop-ins from the open internet.

Same-gender matching

Choose to be matched only with people of your own gender, in every room, pod, and group.

Report and block

Every room has a one-tap report and block. Anyone who breaks the community rules is removed.

You control your camera

Cameras are part of the room, but you choose when to sit down and when to check out.

Questions

Is PreRounds really free?

Yes. Joining the waitlist and using the study rooms are free for students, with no trial period and no paid upgrade.

Do I need a credit card?

No. We only ask for your email and where you are in your path to medicine. We never collect payment information.

Can I study only with women, or only with men?

Yes. Choose "Same gender only" in your preferences, and everyone you're matched with, in every room, pod, and group, will share your gender.

Who runs PreRounds?

PreRounds was started by D.A., who studied for the MCAT alone after graduation and wanted a better place to work. It's built for students, by someone on the same path.

Do I have to be on camera?

Cameras stay on during sessions because seeing others at work is what keeps everyone focused. Microphones stay off on the quiet floor.

When does it open?

PreRounds is opening in stages. Waitlist members get access first, and we'll email you when your spot is ready.

Become a founding member

PreRounds is opening in stages. Founding members get first access and help decide which floors open first. Free, and no card required.
