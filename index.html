Three floors. Pick your focus.

Like a campus library, every floor has its own noise level. Sit wherever matches your energy that day, or book a private room for your partner or study group.

Floor directory

  1. 3 Quiet floorCameras on, microphones off. Silent, focused work.
  2. 2 Collaborative floorBrief check-ins and quick questions between blocks.
  3. 1 Social floorConversation on breaks, focused study in between.
  4. Study roomsBook a 1-on-1 partner, a pod of 2–4, or a group of 5–6.

Every floor meets online, on video, with drop-in tables on each one.

How a session works

  1. Pick a floor or a room

    Join an open table on the quiet, collaborative, or social floor, or book a private room ahead of time.

  2. Get matched

    Partners are paired by time, format, social level, and stage of training.

  3. Set today's goal

    Share your goal in fifteen seconds when you sit down, then check it off before you leave.

  4. Watch your progress

    Hours and goals are logged for you, so you can see how far you've come week to week.

Free. No card. No catch.

Future doctors already pay for prep books, practice exams, the MCAT, and every application along the way. A place to study shouldn't be one more bill.

Nothing to pay

Study rooms, matching, check-ins, and tracking are free for students. There is no paid tier to unlock.

No card to join

Signing up takes your email and nothing else. We never ask for a credit card or payment details.

No cancellation fees

Miss a session and nothing is charged. A reliability score for showing up keeps everyone accountable instead.

Safe by design

We only ask for your email to join, but that's where our safety measures begin, not where they end. Every room is built so you can focus on your work, not on who's on the other side of the screen.

Members only

Every member verifies their email before joining a room. No anonymous drop-ins from the open internet.

Screened sign-ups

Every sign-up is screened for bots and spam before it ever reaches a room.

Encrypted, private sessions

Video and audio are encrypted in transit, and sessions are never recorded by PreRounds.

Same-gender matching

Choose to study only with people of your own gender, in every room, pod, and group.

Report and block

Every room has a one-tap report and block. Anyone who breaks the community rules is removed.

You control your camera

Cameras are part of the room, but you choose when to sit down and when to check out.

Why PreRounds exists

“I studied for the MCAT after graduation, and I learned that the hardest part isn’t the content. It’s showing up, day after day. You don’t need someone to hold you accountable at the finish line. You need peers who hold you accountable in the small moments. That’s what PreRounds is for.”

D.A.Founder, PreRounds
