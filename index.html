The study room for future doctors.

A virtual study room where future physicians work together on camera, from wherever they are. Pick a quiet floor, sit at a drop-in table, or book a room, and get matched by schedule, format, and stage.

Free to use. Waitlist members get first access.

Floor directory

  1. 3 Quiet floorCameras on, microphones off. Silent, focused work.
  2. 2 Collaborative floorBrief check-ins and quick questions between blocks.
  3. 1 Social floorConversation on breaks, focused study in between.
  4. Study roomsBook a 1-on-1 partner, a pod of 2–4, or a group of 5–6.

Every floor meets online, on video. Drop-in tables on each one.

How a session works

  1. Choose where to sit

    Drop in at an open table on any floor, or book a room ahead of time.

  2. Get matched

    Partners are paired by time, format, social level, and stage of training.

  3. Check in

    State your goal in fifteen seconds when you sit down, then mark it done when you leave.

  4. Keep the record

    Hours and goals are logged for you, so progress over weeks is easy to see.
