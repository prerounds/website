Now open: the Founding Study Hall

Study on camera with other future doctors.

A free, silent study session on the Quiet floor. Cameras on, microphones off, and a shared timer that keeps everyone working in focused blocks.

When

  • Thursdays, 9 AM–11 AM ET
  • Thursdays, 8 PM–10 PM ET

Doors open 10 minutes early.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your email. We send a one-time link, and our servers verify it, so only verified members get in.
  2. Come in when the doors open, ten minutes before the session.
  3. Set one goal, turn your camera on, and work in 50-minute blocks with 10-minute breaks.

Your privacy in the room

  • Only members in the session can see you. Everyone signs in with a verified email. Our servers check that sign-in before issuing a private access pass to the room. There's no public link, and no one can watch from outside.
  • Passes expire. Each pass is made for one member and one session, and it stops working shortly after the session ends, so it can't be shared or reused later.
  • Video is encrypted. It travels encrypted through secure video servers, so no one snooping on the internet can view it.
  • Nothing is recorded. PreRounds never records sessions, and no footage is saved anywhere.
  • You're in control. Turn your camera off or leave anytime. Microphones stay off on the Quiet floor.
  • Recording others is against the rules. Anyone can report a member, and the host can remove them.

Sign in to join

Free. No password and no card. The link signs you in on this device.

You're signed in

Our servers verify your sign-in and issue you a private pass for this session only. Only members in the room can see your camera. Video is encrypted and never recorded, and you can turn your camera off or leave anytime.

Room rules

  • Microphones stay off. This is the Quiet floor.
  • Keep your camera appropriate for a shared study space.
  • No recording or screenshots of other members. PreRounds never records sessions.
  • Anyone can report a member from the room, and the host can remove anyone who breaks the rules.
