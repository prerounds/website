Now open: the Founding Study Hall

Study on camera with other future doctors.

A free, silent study session on the Quiet floor. Cameras on, microphones off, and a shared timer that keeps everyone working in focused blocks.

When

  • Thursdays, 9 AM–11 AM ET
  • Thursdays, 8 PM–10 PM ET

Doors open 10 minutes early.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your email. We send a one-time link, so only verified members get in.
  2. Come in when the doors open, ten minutes before the session.
  3. Set one goal, turn your camera on, and work in 50-minute blocks with 10-minute breaks.

Sign in to join

Free. No password and no card. The link signs you in on this device.

You're signed in

Room rules

  • Microphones stay off. This is the Quiet floor.
  • Keep your camera appropriate for a shared study space.
  • No recording or screenshots of other members. PreRounds never records sessions.
  • Anyone can report a member from the room, and the host can remove anyone who breaks the rules.
