Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026

PreRounds ("we," "us") is an independent, student-led project based in Baltimore, Maryland. This policy explains what information we collect through prerounds.org, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions? Email hello@prerounds.org.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address and your stage (for example, post-bacc or applying). You can also share your first name and your study preferences, such as noise level, format, hours, and matching preference.
  • How you found us. If you arrive from one of our links or ads, we record which campaign brought you (for example, "instagram"). This is not linked to your social media account.
  • Basic visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views in aggregate. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.

How we use it

  • To email you about PreRounds, including early access, your invitation, and launch updates.
  • To match you with study partners and rooms that fit your preferences.
  • To understand which outreach is working, so we can reach more students.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We never ask for payment information. PreRounds is free.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this site.

Who helps us run PreRounds

We use a small number of trusted service providers that process data only to provide their services to us: Supabase (database), Vercel (website hosting and analytics), and Zoho (email). They are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@prerounds.org. Every email we send includes a way to stop receiving them.

Keeping your information safe

This site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and access to our database is restricted. The public sign-up form can add a sign-up but cannot read the list.

Age

PreRounds is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has signed up, please contact us and we will delete their information.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know by email.
